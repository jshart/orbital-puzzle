ORBITAL PUZZLE // FLIGHT CREW BRIEFING & PROCEDURES
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Launch your spacecraft from rotating planetary surfaces, exploit orbital momentum, execute gravitational slingshots (flybys), and rendezvous with target orbital waypoints (stations, beacons, and gates) across the solar system.

⚡ WANT A DEMO? (EXAMPLE SOLUTIONS)

To see any mission solved live, open the FLIGHT ADVISORY panel on the right side of the screen: click SHOW HINT, then click AUTO-DIAL SOLUTION ⚡. This automatically dials in the verified launch window and booster thrust. Then hit LAUNCH ROCKET [L] (or ▶ SWEEP) to run the simulation demo!

THE 3-STEP LAUNCH WORKFLOW
1
Launch Window Timing (Scrubber)

Planetary axial rotation gives your rocket a powerful tangential velocity boost (v_surface_spin). Use the Launch Window scrubber at the bottom to rotate your launch pad toward the ejection corridor. The system starts paused—step manually with ◀ / ▶ (or A / D), or click ▶ SWEEP / SPACE to run the sweep automatically.

2
Booster Impulse Configuration

Dial your engine thrust (10 to 65 m/s) using the step buttons or thrust slider. Combined with planetary spin and parent orbital motion, this determines your ejection vector.

3
Trajectory Analysis & Slingshots

Analyze the predictive trajectory path before ignition:

  • Chrono Time Pips (+5s, +10s, +15s): Visual rulers on both your flight path and target orbit to align rendezvous timing at a glance.
  • Arrival Node Ghost: Hologram at closest approach displaying intercept distance and relative velocity (Δv_rel).

RENDEZVOUS & DOCKING
★ Passive Clamp Capture

Entering the target waypoint's Sphere of Influence (green pulsing ring) with relative speed at or below the capture limit automatically locks docking clamps.

⚡ Target-Relative Braking

Arriving inside the SOI with excess speed? Hold SPACE or S inside the SOI to fire RCS thrusters directly opposing your relative drift until capture velocity is reached.

MID-FLIGHT RCS TRIM

Fine-tune your flight path in active flight: hold W / P for Prograde Trim (accelerate forward to expand orbit) or hold S / O for Retrograde Trim (burn backward to drop orbit).
